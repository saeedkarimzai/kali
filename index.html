Welcome to Kali Search
Try “what's one plus one?” or search for something online. Answers you save are kept in this browser and work offline.

  
Save a result to your offline knowledge

  
Offline answers work without Wi-Fi. Mount your USB folder in File Manager and Kali Search automatically updates kali-search-knowledge.json there after every answer. You can also export/import the offline brain manually. DuckDuckGo instant answers need internet. Full web results open on DuckDuckGo because many search engines block embedding inside other websites.





Offline Video Library
Choose videos from your device or USB to build your offline library.
Videos are copied into this browser's local storage for offline playback after import. Large files need enough free browser storage. Supported formats depend on Chrome.
