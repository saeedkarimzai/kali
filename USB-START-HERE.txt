KALI USB EDITION - START HERE

1. On GitHub, open the Kali repository.
2. Click Code, then Download ZIP.
3. Extract the ZIP using your Chromebook Files app.
4. Copy the extracted kali-main folder to your USB drive.
5. Open the USB drive, open kali-main, then open index.html in Chrome.

You can copy only index.html if you want the smallest possible portable desktop. It is a self-contained webpage and does not need Wi-Fi to open.

KALI SEARCH OFFLINE KNOWLEDGE:
- Built-in answers and saved answers work without Wi-Fi.
- In Kali Search, click EXPORT OFFLINE BRAIN and save kali-search-backup.json to this USB.
- On another computer, open Kali and click IMPORT OFFLINE BRAIN to load that backup.
- Export again whenever you want to carry your newest saved answers.

NOTE:
The search engine cannot fetch new web answers without internet. Your saved and built-in knowledge remains available offline. The app itself is tiny and fits easily on a 32 GB USB drive (and should also fit on a 32 MB drive).


AUTOMATIC SEARCH SAVING DIRECTLY TO USB:
1. Plug in your USB drive.
2. Open Kali's File Manager.
3. Click "Mount real folder" and choose a folder on the USB drive.
4. Search answers in Kali Search as usual.
5. Kali automatically writes/updates kali-search-knowledge.json in the mounted USB folder. No manual export is needed while the folder is mounted and permission remains available.
6. On another computer, mount that USB folder and choose IMPORT OFFLINE BRAIN to load the saved knowledge into that browser.

The browser still keeps a local copy too. DuckDuckGo instant-answer results can be saved automatically; full DuckDuckGo pages opened in a separate tab cannot be read automatically by the simulator.
